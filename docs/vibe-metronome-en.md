# Pocket Vibe Metronome Privacy Policy

[日本語](vibe-metronome)

Last updated: November 19, 2026

## 1. App Overview

Pocket Vibe Metronome is a metronome app that communicates tempo through device vibration, click sounds, and visual cues. Click sounds can be turned off. The app includes a foreground-only display mode designed for use while the device is in a pocket.

## 2. Information Collected or Transmitted

The app itself does not include account registration or any feature that asks you to enter or submit personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Google may automatically collect or process the following information for purposes such as ad delivery, performance measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location
- App usage and interaction data, such as app launches and ad impressions or clicks
- Diagnostic information, such as SDK or app performance, crashes, and other operational data
- Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device- or account-related identifiers
- Device, operating system, and other technical information required for ad delivery

The information actually processed may vary depending on your region, device and operating system settings, Google settings, the types of ads delivered, and your consent choices. For more information about how Google handles data, please review:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

For convenience, the app stores the following settings locally on your device:

- BPM
- Time signature
- Count-in settings
- Vibration settings
- Visual display settings
- Keep-screen-awake setting
- Theme setting

These app settings are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to the developer or to third-party servers. However, information related to ad delivery, as described in the previous section, may be transmitted to Google.

## 4. Third-Party Services and External Data Transmission

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and sends Google the information required to make ad requests. Google may process information for ad delivery, ad personalization, performance measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

The app does not use analytics SDKs other than Google AdMob, cloud synchronization, or login features.

## 5. Advertising and Privacy Settings

Depending on your region, device settings, Google advertising settings, and consent choices, the app may display personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. You may be able to manage ad personalization through your device or Google Account advertising settings.

In regions where required by law or Google policy, the app must provide a consent screen or privacy settings for the use of information related to advertising. At release, the developer confirms that consent management meets the requirements applicable to the actual distribution regions.

## 6. Permissions

On Android, the app uses the normal permission `android.permission.VIBRATE` to operate device vibration. This permission is used only for the vibration metronome feature and vibration testing.

On iOS, the app uses device haptic feedback. The app does not use permissions for notifications, background audio, location, microphone, camera, contacts, or similar data.

## 7. Data Deletion

You can restore locally stored settings to their defaults by using "Reset Settings" in the app. Uninstalling the app also deletes settings stored locally on the device.

The app does not create accounts, so it does not provide an account deletion feature. To manage or delete information that Google processes in connection with ad delivery, please use the privacy settings on your device and in your Google Account.

## 8. Important Notes

- The app communicates tempo through vibration, click sounds, and visual cues. Click sounds can be turned off.
- Although the app is designed for use while the device is in a pocket, use it only when the device can be held safely.
- The app operates only while it is in the foreground.
- Playback stops when the app moves to the background or the screen is locked.
- Vibration strength, duration, and feel may vary depending on the device and operating system settings.
- Timing may vary depending on the operating system and device state; exact timing accuracy is not guaranteed.
- The app is not a medical device, health management device, or professional measuring instrument.

If changes to the app affect how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the revision date will be shown.
